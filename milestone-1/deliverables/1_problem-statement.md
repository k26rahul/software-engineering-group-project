# Problem Statement

## Application Overview

We are building a **Library Management System** for the Rashtrotthana Vidya Kendra (RVK) campus library. This web application will transition the library from manual, paper-based operations — physical cards, registers, and verbal requests — into an automated, digital workflow system. The platform will manage memberships, book catalogs, issue/return cycles, reservation queues, budget tracking, and community features like book reviews and recommendations.

## The Current Landscape

- **Location:** Rashtrotthana Vidya Kendra (RVK) campus, which houses four distinct schools.
- **Inventory:** Approximately 5,000 books in the library catalog.
- **User Base:**
  - ~1,500 active campus students.
  - ~600 registered off-campus members from the outside community.

## The Core Problem

- **100% Paper-Based Operations:** Every workflow — issuing books, tracking returns, managing reservations, and tallying the monthly budget — is handled manually across multiple physical registers.
- **Severe Administrative Overload:** Managing 2,100+ members and 5,000+ books on paper forces the librarian to work through Sundays just to keep records accurate.
- **Poor Experience for Members:** Members cannot search the catalog digitally, there is no formal reservation system, and they are forced to return all their books just to renew an expired card.

## The Digital Transformation Impact

### For the Librarian

- **Manual Tracking** ➔ **Automated Management:** Eliminates the overhead of managing 2,100+ members and 5,000+ books across multiple physical registers.
- **Administrative Burnout** ➔ **Freed Capacity:** Removes the need for weekend catch-up work, giving the librarian her time back.
- **Clerical Data Entry** ➔ **Creative Focus:** Frees the librarian to invest time in interacting with readers, curating reading lists, moderating reviews, and reading herself.

### For Members

- **Rack-by-Rack Search** ➔ **Instant Digital Discovery:** Members can find any book in seconds by searching the catalog instead of physically browsing racks.
- **Verbal Reservation Requests** ➔ **Automated, Fair Waitlist:** A formal digital queue replaces the informal, memory-based system that leads to confusion and unfair outcomes.
- **Forced Full-Return Card Renewals** ➔ **Continuous Digital Access:** Members no longer need to return all their books just to renew a card. Their digital account continues without disruption.
