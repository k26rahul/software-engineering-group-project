## Discussion Summary

- The team discussed the necessity of proving that the selected tech stack (React, Vite, Tailwind on the frontend and FastAPI, SQLite on the backend) works together seamlessly in practice.
- Harikesh pointed out that starting feature development from an empty repository would lead to massive merge conflicts.
- It was agreed that a foundational "Version Zero" skeleton is required. This skeleton will include the base folder structure, dependencies, and a minimal working connection between the frontend and backend.
- Rahul and Vidhatri volunteered to take on the responsibility of setting up this Version Zero.
- Once Version Zero is merged into the `main` branch, the rest of the team will be able to branch off and begin parallel development of their assigned backlog items.

## Decisions Taken

- Assigned the responsibility of creating the "Version Zero" skeleton to Rahul and Vidhatri.
- Decided that no feature branches will be created until Version Zero is successfully merged into `main`.
