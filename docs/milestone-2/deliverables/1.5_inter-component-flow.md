# Inter-Component Flow

## How Components Interact (Inter-Component Flow)

This document traces 10 representative workflows across the three tiers (Frontend → Backend → Database) of the Library Management System. Together, these workflows touch all 9 functional modules and the cross-cutting notification concern, demonstrating how the components defined in the sub-documents connect end-to-end.

| #   | Workflow                                             | Module(s) Covered                                               |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Member Issues a Book                                 | Circulation                                                     |
| 2   | Book is Returned with Active Reservation             | Circulation, Reservation & Request, Notifications               |
| 3   | Member Self-Registration, Approval & Fee Payment     | Authentication & Identity, Membership Management, Notifications |
| 4   | Member Places a Reservation on an Unavailable Book   | Reservation & Request                                           |
| 5   | Overdue Detection & Automated Reminder Notifications | Circulation, Notifications                                      |
| 6   | Librarian Adds a Book via ISBN Barcode Scan          | Catalog & Inventory                                             |
| 7   | Member Purchase Request, Upvoting & Fulfillment      | Reservation & Request                                           |
| 8   | Subscription Management & Monthly Budget Tracking    | Budget & Subscription                                           |
| 9   | Review Submission, Moderation & Recommendations      | Community & Curation, Recommendations                           |
| 10  | Campus Management Reviews Usage & Expense Reports    | Reporting, Budget & Subscription                                |

### Workflow 1: Member Issues a Book

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Member clicks 'Issue' on BorrowingDashboardPage"] --> B["Frontend: IssuePage"]
    B -- "HTTP POST" --> C["Backend: circulation_router<br>(/transactions/issue)"]
    C --> D["CirculationService"]
    D --> E["CatalogService<br>(check availability)"]
    D --> F["MemberService<br>(check fines)"]
    D --> G["ReservationService<br>(check reservation)"]
    E --> H["BookCopy<br>(model)"]
    F --> I["User / Fine<br>(models)"]
    G --> J["Reservation<br>(model)"]
    H --> K["Transaction (model)<br>← new record created"]
```

### Workflow 2: Book is Returned with Active Reservation

flowchart TD

### Workflow 3: Member Self-Registration, Librarian Approval & Fee Payment

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Member fills registration form<br>(name, email, password, membership_type)"] --> B["Frontend: RegistrationPage"]
    B -- "HTTP POST" --> C["Backend: auth_router<br>(/auth/register)"]
    C --> D["AuthService.register()"]
    D --> E["User (model)<br>← created with approval_status=PENDING"]

    F["Librarian opens MemberApprovalPage"] --> G["Frontend: MemberApprovalPage"]
    G -- "HTTP PATCH" --> H["Backend: members_router<br>(/members/{id}/approve)"]
    H --> I["MemberService.approve_member()"]
    I --> J["User (model)<br>← approval_status=APPROVED, is_active=true"]
    I --> K["NotificationService.notify_registration_status()"]
    K --> L["EmailClient<br>(sends approval email)"]
    K --> M["Notification (model)<br>← REGISTRATION_APPROVED"]

    N["Off-campus member opens PaymentsPage<br>to pay ₹100 annual fee"] --> O["Frontend: PaymentsPage"]
    O -- "HTTP POST" --> P["Backend: payments_router<br>(/payments)"]
    P --> Q["PaymentService.process_membership_renewal()"]
    Q --> R["MemberService<br>(update membership_start_date)"]
    Q --> S["Payment (model)<br>← created, type=MEMBERSHIP_FEE"]
```

### Workflow 4: Member Places a Reservation on an Unavailable Book

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Member views BookDetailPage<br>sees 'All copies issued'"] --> B["Member clicks 'Reserve' button"]
    B --> C["Frontend: BookDetailPage"]
    C -- "HTTP POST" --> D["Backend: reservations_router<br>(/reservations)"]
    D --> E["ReservationService.place_reservation()"]
    E --> F["CatalogService<br>(verify book exists, availability=0)"]
    F --> G["Book / BookCopy<br>(models)"]
    E --> H["Reservation (model)<br>← created, status=WAITING,<br>queue_position assigned"]

    I["Member opens MyReservationsPage"] --> J["Frontend: MyReservationsPage"]
    J -- "HTTP GET" --> K["Backend: reservations_router<br>(/members/{id}/reservations)"]
    K --> L["ReservationService.get_member_reservations()"]
    L --> M["ReservationService.get_queue_position()"]
    M --> H
```

### Workflow 5: Overdue Detection & Automated Reminder Notifications

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Librarian opens OverdueListPage<br>(or scheduled job triggers)"] --> B["Frontend: OverdueListPage"]
    B -- "HTTP GET" --> C["Backend: circulation_router<br>(/transactions/overdue)"]
    C --> D["CirculationService.get_overdue_items()"]
    D --> E["Transaction (model)<br>← queried via is_overdue() / days_overdue()"]
    D --> F["NotificationService.send_overdue_reminders()"]
    F --> G["EmailClient<br>(sends reminder email per member)"]
    F --> H["Notification (model)<br>← created, type=OVERDUE_REMINDER"]
    H --> I["Member sees NotificationBanner<br>on MemberDashboard / NotificationsPage"]
```

### Workflow 6: Librarian Adds a Book to Catalog via ISBN Barcode Scan

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Librarian scans ISBN on CatalogManagementPage"] --> B["Frontend: BarcodeScannerInput<br>(component)"]
    B -- "HTTP POST" --> C["Backend: catalog_router<br>(/books/isbn-lookup)"]
    C --> D["CatalogService.lookup_isbn()"]
    D --> E["ExternalISBNClient<br>(queries external ISBN database)"]
    E --> F["Title, author, genre<br>auto-filled in form"]
    F --> G["Librarian reviews & submits form"]
    G -- "HTTP POST" --> H["Backend: catalog_router<br>(/books)"]
    H --> I["CatalogService.add_book()"]
    I --> J["Book (model)<br>← created"]
    I --> K["CatalogService.add_copies()"]
    K --> L["BookCopy (model)<br>← created, status=AVAILABLE"]
```

### Workflow 7: Member Purchase Request, Community Upvoting & Fulfillment

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Member submits request on BookRequestPage<br>(title, author, description)"] --> B["Frontend: BookRequestPage"]
    B -- "HTTP POST" --> C["Backend: requests_router<br>(/purchase-requests)"]
    C --> D["PurchaseRequestService.submit_request()"]
    D --> E["PurchaseRequest (model)<br>← created, status=PENDING"]

    F["Other members browse & upvote"] --> G["Frontend: BookRequestPage"]
    G -- "HTTP POST" --> H["Backend: requests_router<br>(/purchase-requests/{id}/upvote)"]
    H --> I["PurchaseRequestService.upvote_request()"]
    I --> J["PurchaseRequestVote (model)<br>← created"]
    I --> K["PurchaseRequest.upvote_count<br>← incremented"]

    L["Librarian opens PurchaseRequestsPage"] --> M["Frontend: PurchaseRequestsPage"]
    M -- "HTTP GET" --> N["Backend: requests_router<br>(/purchase-requests)"]
    N --> O["PurchaseRequestService.get_ranked_requests()"]
    O --> K

    P["Librarian buys book & marks fulfilled"] --> Q["Frontend: PurchaseRequestsPage"]
    Q -- "HTTP PATCH" --> R["Backend: requests_router<br>(/purchase-requests/{id}/purchased)"]
    R --> S["PurchaseRequestService.mark_as_purchased()"]
    S --> T["PurchaseRequest (model)<br>← status=PURCHASED"]
```

### Workflow 9: Review Submission, Moderation & Recommendation Update

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Member submits rating & review<br>on BookDetailPage"] --> B["Frontend: BookDetailPage"]
    B -- "HTTP POST" --> C["Backend: reviews_router<br>(/books/{id}/reviews)"]
    C --> D["ReviewService.submit_review()"]
    D --> E["Review (model)<br>← created, status=PENDING"]

    F["Librarian opens ReviewModerationPage"] --> G["Frontend: ReviewModerationPage"]
    G -- "HTTP GET" --> H["Backend: reviews_router<br>(pending reviews)"]
    H --> I["ReviewService.get_pending_reviews()"]
    I --> E

    J["Librarian approves review"] --> K["Frontend: ReviewModerationPage"]
    K -- "HTTP PATCH" --> L["Backend: reviews_router<br>(/reviews/{id}/moderate)"]
    L --> M["ReviewService.moderate_review()"]
    M --> N["Review (model)<br>← status=APPROVED"]
    N --> O["Displayed on BookDetailPage<br>(public reviews section)"]

    P["Member opens MemberDashboard"] --> Q["Frontend: MemberDashboard<br>(recommendations section)"]
    Q -- "HTTP GET" --> R["Backend: recommendations_router<br>(/members/{id}/recommendations)"]
    R --> S["RecommendationService.get_recommendations()"]
    S --> T["RecommendationService.analyze_reading_preferences()"]
    T --> U["Transaction (model)<br>← borrowing history read"]
    S --> V["Book (model)<br>← matching genres/authors, unread"]
```

### Workflow 10: Campus Management Reviews Usage & Expense Reports

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Campus Management opens UsageReportsPage"] --> B["Frontend: UsageReportsPage"]
    B -- "HTTP GET" --> C["Backend: reports_router<br>(/reports/usage)"]
    C --> D["ReportService.get_usage_report()"]
    D --> E["Transaction / User (models)<br>← aggregated for active members,<br>books issued/month, popular categories"]

    F["Campus Management opens ExpenseReportPage"] --> G["Frontend: ExpenseReportPage"]
    G -- "HTTP GET" --> H["Backend: reports_router<br>(/reports/expense)"]
    H --> I["ReportService.get_expense_report()"]
    I --> J["BudgetService.generate_expense_report()"]
    J --> K["BookPurchaseEntry / Subscription<br>(models)<br>← aggregated by category & vendor"]
    K --> L["Itemized report displayed<br>(newspapers, magazines, books, vendors)"]

    M["Campus Management clicks 'Download'"] --> N["Frontend: ExpenseReportPage /<br>UsageReportsPage"]
    N -- "HTTP GET" --> O["Backend: reports_router<br>(/reports/download/{type})"]
    O --> P["ReportService.download_report()"]
    P --> Q["Returns downloadable file bytes<br>(for reimbursement approval & record-keeping)"]
```
