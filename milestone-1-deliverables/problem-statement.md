# Problem Statement

## Application Overview
We are building a comprehensive **Library Management System** specifically designed for the Rashtrotthana Vidya Kendra (RVK) campus library. This web application will transition the library from manual, paper-based operations (using physical cards, registers, and verbal requests) into an automated, digital workflow system. The platform will manage memberships, book catalogs, issue/return cycles, reservation queues, and community engagement features like book reviews.

## The Current Landscape
*   **Location:** Rashtrotthana Vidya Kendra (RVK) campus, which houses four distinct schools.
*   **Inventory:** Approximately 5,000 books in the library catalog.
*   **User Base:** 
    *   ~1,500 active campus students.
    *   ~600 registered off-campus members from the outside community.

## The Core Problem
*   **100% Paper-Based Operations:** Every single workflow—from issuing books and tracking renewals to managing the budget—is done manually across various physical registers.
*   **Severe Administrative Overload:** The immense volume of manual tracking forces the librarian to work through Sundays, spending entire days documenting, arranging, and cross-checking records to maintain accuracy.

## The Digital Transformation Impact
Implementing an automated system will directly solve these operational bottlenecks:
*   **Manual Tracking** ➔ **Automated Management:** Eliminates the mental pressure and extreme time-cost of managing 2,100+ members and 5,000+ books on paper.
*   **Administrative Burnout** ➔ **Freed Capacity:** Returns precious time to the librarian, removing the need for weekend administrative catch-up.
*   **Clerical Data Entry** ➔ **Community Engagement:** Empowers the librarian to shift focus from data entry to high-value tasks, such as:
    *   Interacting directly with learners and readers.
    *   Curating and organizing reading lists.
    *   Reading and moderating community book reviews.
    *   Investing time into reading the books herself.
